PUBLISH

Your key, your tool, one reviewer.

The console creates an Ed25519 key, you enrol, and you publish from the workspace. Every request is signed; nothing enters the catalog without a clean scan, sandbox tests and an operator's approval.

/tools keygen /tools enroll acme acme_* --contact ops@acme.example /tools publish acme_count → scanned · no findings · tests passed
