Catalog and registry

Pre-forged tools in the exact shape of a forged tool, served from a signed static index at registry.underpassai.com. Install from the console; publish with your own Ed25519 key after an operator approves your enrolment. Every entry: policy scan, sandbox tests, a named reviewer.

PUBLISH IN FOUR LINES
/tools keygen /tools enroll acme acme_* --contact ops@acme.example /tools publish acme_count → scanned · no findings · tests passed

Signed requests, per-key rate limits, no tokens. The operator approves the publisher once; every version is reviewed on its own.
